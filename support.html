Stipple Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea: we read every message.

Write to us
In the app: Settings › Send feedback. It opens an email with your iOS and app version filled in, which you see before sending.

My habits don’t show up on my other iPhone or iPad

Turn on Settings › iCloud & data › iCloud Sync on both, signed in to the same Apple Account. The first sync can take a few minutes; keep Stipple open on the device that has the data.

I’m away or ill. Will I lose my progress?

No. Open the habit, tap More, then Pause…. Paused days don’t count against anything, and every dot you already have stays.

How do I stop a habit without deleting it?

Open the habit, tap More, then Archive…. Archived habits keep their history. To bring one back, touch and hold + on Today, choose Arrange Today…, then Archived.

How do I keep a copy of my data?

Settings › iCloud & data › Export a copy saves a file you control. Restore from a file brings it back, on this iPhone or a new one.

Reminders don’t arrive

Check that notifications for Stipple are allowed in the iOS Settings app, and that a Focus isn’t holding them. A habit that’s already done today, or paused, isn’t reminded.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › iCloud & data › Delete all data removes everything from your iPhone, and from iCloud when sync is on.

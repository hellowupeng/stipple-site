Stipple Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Stipple doesn’t collect your data. There is no account, there are no ads, and nothing you put in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your iPhone

Your habits, entries, moods, notes and photos are stored on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud Sync, they are also stored in your own iCloud account’s private database, which only you can read. We never see them.

Apple Watch, widgets and Siri

Your Apple Watch, widgets, Control Center and Siri read the same data from your iPhone. Nothing leaves your devices on the way.

Usage and diagnostics

Stipple can note how it is used (for example, that a timer was started) and iOS can hand it crash, hang and launch-time reports. These stay on your iPhone. They only leave it if you choose to: behavior events go into an export only when you include them, and Send feedback mentions crash and launch figures in one line of an email you see and send yourself. In test (TestFlight) builds that email also lists a few counts, such as how many days you used the app in your first week; you see them and can delete them before sending.

Apple Health

If you connect a habit to Apple Health, Stipple reads only the kinds of data you allow (for example steps or workouts) to fill in that habit, on your iPhone. Health data is never sent to us or used for anything else, and you can turn access off at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Stipple asks for permission only when you turn a reminder on.

Exports and backups

An export, and the weekly copy Stipple keeps in Files on your iPhone, are files you control. Stipple never sends them anywhere.

Purchases

Stipple+ is bought and renewed through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; Stipple only learns whether Stipple+ is active on your Apple Account, and never sees your name, email or payment details. Manage or cancel it in the iOS Settings app › your name › Subscriptions.

Deleting your data

Settings › iCloud & data › Delete all data removes everything from your iPhone, and from iCloud when sync is on. Deleting the app removes what is on your iPhone.

Children

Stipple is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: use Settings › Send feedback in the app, or see Support.
